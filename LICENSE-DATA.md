The shortlist dumps and per-query records are derived from Discogs-VI, whose
metadata is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. They are therefore released under the
same licence:

- **Attribution.** Cite Araz, Serra and Bogdanov, *Discogs-VI: A musical
  version identification dataset based on public editorial metadata*, ISMIR
  2024, alongside this work.
- **NonCommercial.** The data may not be used for commercial purposes.
- **ShareAlike.** Derivatives of the data must carry the same licence.

No audio is distributed here, and none can be reconstructed from these files:
they contain track indices, distances and clique identifiers only.
